Drop your 2 photos in this folder, named exactly:

image1.jpg
image2.jpg

(All lowercase "image", no spaces, .jpg extension — if yours are
.png or .jpeg, either rename them to .jpg or edit the `src` line in
script.js's buildGallery() function to match.)

Until a real photo is added, that card will just show a small pink
placeholder with "add assets/imageN.jpg" — so you can preview the whole
site before you've dropped the photos in.
